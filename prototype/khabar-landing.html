Why it goes wrong at home

The note is written in Latin shorthand. The patient reads BM, 中文 or தமிழ்.

Doctors in busy clinics have minutes per patient. What goes home is a note like the one above, and a patient left to guess which tablet goes with which meal.

Before the visit

Three clinics. One kitchen table. One hidden duplicate.

Aminah photographs every packet she takes, including jamu and supplements. Khabar matches each brand to its actual medicine and checks them against each other before the doctor writes anything new.
